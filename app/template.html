Bars: countries on WARNING (red) or WATCH (amber) each week. White ticks: a new outbreak began in a quiet country. Space plays or pauses; arrow keys step.
1 · Ingest public data

WHO weekly cholera cases and outbreak news, GDACS droughts and floods, NOAA El Niño index, HDX conflict, displacement and food-security aggregates.

2 · Detect surges

A country surges when its last 2 weeks hold at least 50 cases and three times its previous 6-week average.

3 · Score quiet countries

Signals: a neighbour surging, an active drought, a conflict spike, El Niño (East Africa). One signal sets WATCH, two or more set WARNING.

4 · Explain and propose

Each alert names its evidence, a proportionate preventive action and the role that acts. A mandated officer decides.

Public, aggregated data

Country-level counts only. No personal or individual health data is collected or shown.

Human in the loop

The system prepares a brief; it never sends public messages or triggers action on its own.

Uncertainty on screen

Every rule shows how often it was right against chance, to limit alert fatigue.

Known limits

National resolution only, uneven reporting between countries, 39 onsets of history: some apparent gains may be luck. Floods and food insecurity are shown as context because they did no better than chance as triggers.

Data: WHO Global Cholera & AWD dashboard, WHO Disease Outbreak News, GDACS, NOAA ONI, HDX HAPI, Open-Meteo ERA5 · Back-test January 2023 – October 2026 · Generated · Human decides, machine prepares.
